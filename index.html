"RTL to GDS. Bare-metal firmware. Silicon to software — I do both."

I'm a Final-Year Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering fresher at Vishwakarma Institute of Technology, Pune — eager to start my career at the intersection of VLSI / RTL design and Embedded Systems.

On the RTL & VLSI side: I design in Verilog, run physical layout in Cadence Virtuoso, prototype on Xilinx FPGAs using Vivado, and have trained multi-agent LLM pipelines for RTL generation. On the Embedded side: I write bare-metal firmware for STM32, ESP32, and PIC18F microcontrollers, design PCBs in KiCAD, and have built industry-sponsored IoT systems using LoRa and wireless charging.

I've led the electronics team at ISA, VIT Pune, completed two industry internships, and co-authored an IEEE paper at ICFT 2025. As a fresher, I'm actively looking for my first full-time role in VLSI design, embedded Electronics, or both.
